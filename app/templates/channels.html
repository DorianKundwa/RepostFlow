Google OAuth & YouTube API — Developer Notes
Google Cloud Project Status: In Production

Because your Google Cloud OAuth consent screen is configured as In Production, your refresh tokens are permanent and will not expire after 7 days. RepostFlow automatically requests fresh 1-hour access tokens behind the scenes, ensuring uninterrupted 24/7 channel monitoring and scheduled reposting.

Why do I still see an "unverified app" warning from Google?

Google displays an unverified app warning for any application requesting sensitive scopes (such as YouTube upload) until a public brand verification audit is conducted. Since RepostFlow is your private self-hosted automation system, you do not need to submit for Google verification. Simply click "Advanced → Go to RepostFlow (unsafe)" when authorising. This is completely safe for your own Google account.

YouTube API quota

YouTube Data API v3 provides 10,000 units per day by default. Each video upload consumes approximately 1,600 units. Monitor your usage in Google Cloud Console → APIs & Services → Quotas.

Brand Account channels

If your YouTube channel is managed as a Brand Account, you may need to log in using the Brand Account's Google sign-in directly, not your personal account. Each Google sign-in grants access to the channels directly owned by that account.
